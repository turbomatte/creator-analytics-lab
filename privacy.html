Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Creator Analytics Lab accesses only TikTok data that a user explicitly authorizes, such as basic profile information, public video information, and video performance statistics.

The data is used solely for personal performance analysis.

Creator Analytics Lab does not sell user data and does not share user data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Access tokens and credentials are intended to be stored locally or in a private environment and should not be publicly disclosed.

Users may revoke TikTok authorization through their TikTok account settings.
